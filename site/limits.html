Risk limits

The desk quotes inside a fixed envelope. Each rule is checked on every tick. When a rule trips, the controller cancels every order and stops quoting until the desk is reset. The watchdog runs in the same process as the quoting loop, so a rule cannot be skipped.

How each rule is enforced
Position and loss

The desk measures net base exposure against the position cap on every tick. Realized plus unrealized loss is tracked from the session open. Crossing either limit cancels all resting orders immediately.

Feed and rate

A timestamp on the last exchange tick drives the stale breaker. If the socket goes quiet past 800 ms, quoting stops and the desk waits for a fresh tick. Order sends are rate-limited to four per second.

Inventory and spread

The desk keeps inventory inside a target band and skews quotes to pull back toward it. When the market spread exceeds six bps, the surface is too thin to quote safely and the desk widens or pulls instead.
